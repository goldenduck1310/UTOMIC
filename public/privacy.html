Clear handling of project information.

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Information collected

When you submit a project enquiry, UTOMIC receives the information you provide, such as your name, email address, optional phone number, project or company name, selected service, project scope, description and goal.

How it is used

This information is used only to review and respond to your enquiry, communicate about the proposed project and protect the enquiry system from abuse.

Processing and delivery

Project enquiries are processed by UTOMIC’s secure Vercel serverless endpoint and delivered using Resend. These providers process the information only as required to operate the website and deliver the enquiry.

Retention and sharing

UTOMIC does not sell enquiry information. It may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain project records or meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask UTOMIC to correct or delete information submitted through the enquiry form by emailing sheehansheehan120@gmail.com.
